Launch!

A solo-developed Roblox experience in the launcher genre. You're shot out of a cannon, and everything after that is momentum management: slam to convert altitude into speed, bounce off enemies, hijack their rockets, planes and fireworks, and hit destructible walls fast enough to break into the next biome.

01

Custom physics simulation

Bespoke velocity integration with per-map gravity, friction, bounciness, speed limits and decay curves — all driven by a shared config that composes base constants with player upgrades and prestige bonuses.

02

Treadmill world streaming

A four-chunk object pool recycled on RenderStep, with hot template swapping so biome transitions happen mid-run with no loading break.

03

State machine architecture

Ten movement states — Flying, Slamming, CloudCrash, WallState and five vehicle states — registered against a central dispatcher.

04

Voxel destruction

A custom voxelizer with an object cache for breakable walls; debris stays synced to the treadmill so nothing drifts out of frame.

05

Cross-server ranked duels

MemoryStore queue + MessagingService pub/sub + TeleportService, with medal-based ELO, match reconciliation and friend challenges.

06

Server-authoritative persistence

A DataStore layer with schema reconciliation, legacy data migration, field normalisation, retry logic, UpdateAsync merge-on-save, autosave and BindToClose flushing.

07

Live-ops stack

Global leaderboards, rotating featured deals, daily login and playtime rewards, redeem codes, rewarded ads and gifting.

08

Analytics instrumentation

Rate-limited funnel tracking across eight progression paths, built for retention and conversion analysis rather than vanity counts.

09

Shared-server multiplayer

Per-player lane assignment with collision groups, so every player runs an independent simulation inside the same server.
