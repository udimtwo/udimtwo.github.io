Launch!

You get shot out of a cannon and everything after is momentum. Slam to turn height into speed, take enemies' rockets and planes, break through walls into the next biome.

  • Custom physics — per-map gravity, friction, bounce, speed caps
  • Treadmill world streaming, four pooled chunks, biomes swapped mid-run
  • Ten movement states on one dispatcher
  • Voxel destruction with an object cache
  • Cross-server ranked duels — MemoryStore, MessagingService, medal-based ELO
  • Server-authoritative saving — schema migration, UpdateAsync merge-on-save, BindToClose flushing

Roblox game

Idle Breakout

A recreation of the Coolmath Games idle classic, built entirely in Roblox's 2D GUI layer — no parts, no character, no imported art.

  • Instance-free simulation — fixed timestep, circle-vs-AABB, grid broadphase
  • Pooled rendering, flat cost at five balls or four hundred
  • Seven ball types and thirteen level layouts held as data
  • Server-authoritative purchases, economy invariant asserted at load

Prototype

Flight

You are the aircraft, not a vehicle. Six-axis character flight over a procedural neon city.

  • Custom flight controller — hold-to-fly, hovering, bank-to-turn, afterburner, speed-scaled lateral thrust
  • Camera bound to input, not the physics body — an AlignOrientation constraint chases attitude on the rigid body, so collisions stay physical
  • Procedural city — 196 towers from a seeded RNG, height falloff, neon trim
  • Chase camera — occlusion raycasting, speed-scaled FOV, scroll zoom

System

Momentum Movement

A first-person controller that replaces Roblox's Humanoid physics entirely, for momentum-based FPS movement in the Source lineage.

  • Humanoid disabled, physics rebuilt — custom gravity, single-box collider, P-controller hover for ground contact and slopes
  • Air strafing, slide chaining, slope acceleration — chains build 22 → ~45 studs/s and converge instead of running away
  • Framerate-independent deceleration — friction and camera springs solved in closed form, identical at 30, 60 and 240 fps
  • First-person camera — speed-scaled FOV, strafe roll, spring-damped landings, with no writes to player aim
