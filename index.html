Roblox game

Launch!

You get shot out of a cannon and everything after that is momentum. Slam to turn height into speed, bounce off enemies, take their rockets and planes, and hit walls hard enough to break into the next biome.

Play Launch!

What's under it

Custom physics

Own velocity integration with per-map gravity, friction, bounce and speed caps, composed with upgrades and prestige bonuses.

Treadmill streaming

The player never moves. Four pooled world chunks recycle past a fixed point on RenderStep, templates hot-swapped for biome changes.

Ten movement states

Flying, slamming, cloud crashes, wall carving and five vehicle states, all on one dispatcher.

Voxel destruction

Custom voxelizer with an object cache for breakable walls; debris stays synced to the treadmill.

Cross-server duels

MemoryStore queue, MessagingService and TeleportService, with medal-based ELO and friend challenges.

Saving that holds up

Schema migration, retry logic, UpdateAsync merge-on-save, autosave and BindToClose flushing.

Live-ops

Global leaderboards, rotating deals, daily rewards, redeem codes, rewarded ads and gifting.

Analytics

Rate-limited funnel tracking across eight progression paths.

Shared servers

Per-player lanes with collision groups, so everyone runs an independent simulation in the same server.
